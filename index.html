Research

I study the single-nucleus genomics of Alzheimer's disease and cognitive resilience, working to bridge network-level and cellular-level accounts of brain aging. My research asks how molecular changes within individual cells relate to the broader breakdown — and preservation — of cognitive function across the aging brain.

I completed my PhD in Cognition and Neuroscience at the University of Texas at Dallas.
